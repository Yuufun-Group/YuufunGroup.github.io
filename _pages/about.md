## 榆欢小组的发展历程

 
- 榆欢小组最初在**2024年**成立，最开始是仅仅是一个研学组织，我们在那年7月制作了第一支研学视频（您或许会在不久后在本网站的博客中见到）。

- 后来我们建成了一个学校新闻组织，这就是榆欢小组的前身，我们首次在这里使用“榆欢”的名字。我们在2024年10月-11月制作了一些新闻内容，在那一段时间里，我们亲自走访了许多同学，关于一些问题问到了同学们的想法，我们依然很怀念当年制作出成品的喜悦（依旧或许您可能在不久后可以看到我们的成果）。我们也在当年建立了第一个网站，却因学业原因废弃了它，但您现在仍可以访问它（即使没什么用，网址：[榆欢小组-Yuufun Group](https://yuufungroup.wordpress.com/)）
-  2025年1月，榆欢小组正式成立，我们的人员也从最初的4位变为7位。2025年2月，我们小组完成了一份80页的幻灯片项目，这是我们榆欢小组制作过的最大的项目之一，我们使用了将近1月多的时间以完成这个作品。这个作品将会放入一个链接中供给下载参考
- 2025年8月，我们又一次制作了两个大项目，其一是关于道德的宣传有趣的默剧，其二则是简介中所有提及到的粤剧文化宣传项目，我们使用了两个月的时间，亲自走访，实践，日行超3万步，我们无惧风雨，在8月份完成了这两个项目，并附有几份讲述文章。
-  **对未来的展望**： 我认为榆欢能有今时今日的成就离不开我们所有人的共同支持和努力，我相信未来的榆欢一定会蓬勃发展得更好！

# Welcome to the Official Website of Yuufun Group!

## **This is a Brief Introduction to the Official Website**

中文网站在上方哦！

Yuufun Group is a learning organization founded by four junior high school students. On this website, we will publish observations and insights from our practical research activities and document our learning achievements in our blog. The purpose of establishing this website/blog is to ensure the effective preservation of our works and make them accessible to the public!

Through the display of our learning outcomes, you may gain some understanding of Chinese culture, such as traditional arts like Cantonese Opera.

----------

## Development History of Yuufun Group

-   Yuufun Group was initially established in **2024** as a pure research organization. In July of that year, we produced our first research video (which you may find in the blog section of this website soon).
    
-   Later, we developed into a school news organization, which served as the predecessor of Yuufun Group. It was at this stage that we first adopted the name "Yuufun". From October to November 2024, we created a series of news content. During that period, we personally interviewed many classmates to gather their opinions on various topics, and we still cherish the joy of completing those works (you may also have the chance to view our achievements soon). We also launched our first website that year, but it was abandoned due to academic pressures. Nevertheless, you can still visit it (though it is no longer updated): [Yuufun Group](https://yuufungroup.wordpress.com/).
    
-   In January 2025, Yuufun Group was officially founded, with our team expanding from the initial 4 members to 7. In February 2025, our group completed an 80-page slideshow project—one of the largest projects we have ever undertaken. It took us over a month to finish this work, which will be available for download and reference via a dedicated link.
    
-   In August 2025, we completed two major projects. The first is an engaging mime performance promoting moral values, and the second is the Cantonese Opera cultural promotion project mentioned in the introduction. Over two months, we conducted on-site visits and practical research, walking more than 30,000 steps every day. Braving wind and rain, we successfully finished both projects in August, accompanied by several supporting articles.
    
-   **Outlook for the Future**: We attribute Yuufun Group’s current achievements to the joint support and efforts of all members. We firmly believe that Yuufun will thrive and achieve even greater success in the future!
